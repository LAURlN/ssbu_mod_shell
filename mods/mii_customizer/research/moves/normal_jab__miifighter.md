### 1.1 Corrections to the earlier decodes

- **The first-ret rule ran past function ends.** The decoder stops at the first `ret`; for `game_attack100`,
  `sound_attack100`, `effect_attack100` and `expression_attack100` the next registered function was included.
  Bounds used: game_attack100 `< 0x158f20`, sound_attack100 `< 0x17a790`, effect_attack100 `< 0xa1470`,
  expression_attack100 `< 0xde030`.
- **The rapid hit is helper 0x158370**: `macros::ATTACK` under its own `is_excute`, then `wait(1)`, then
  `if is_excute { clear_all; on_flag(0x77c) }`. `0x77c` = `FIGHTER_STATUS_ATTACK_FLAG_100_CONTINUE_CHECK`
  (constmap_local). Called at frames 1, 3, ..., 15 and at the loop head after frame 17.
- **`game_attack100start` has no registration**: the hash of `game_attack100start` (mid 16 bits 0x8e3b, lo 0x5d31,
  hi 0x13) has no `movk`/`movz` site in the NRO text (tier B). The Brawler's rapid start has sound and expression
  scripts only.
- **`kneel` is the Gunner's KneeL bone**: Smash hash40 is case-insensitive; the skeleton's `KneeL` hashes to the
  same value as `kneel` (0x5e1ab8a2b, tier B).
- **The Brawler's sequence records play only voice tones.** The children of `seq_miifighter_rnd_attack100` and
  `_02` resolve to `vc_mii_attack04/05/06/100` (CRC32 match). The CRC 0xeb86b427 in both records also occurs in

## 3. Statuses

- `FIGHTER_STATUS_KIND_ATTACK` (0x27): `Init` runs the vanilla Init, then for a Gunner whose Mii chose `normal_jab`,
  `start_attack` swaps a vanilla `attack_11`/`attack_12` for the owned stage. `Main` runs the vanilla Main, then
  `attack_main`, which swaps a vanilla `attack_11`/`attack_12` set in place by the common status.
- `FIGHTER_STATUS_KIND_ATTACK_100` (0x28): `Init` and `Main` run the vanilla function, then `attack_100_main`, which
  swaps a vanilla `attack_100_start`/`attack_100`/`attack_100_end` for the owned motion.
- A swap happens only when the current motion kind equals a vanilla name and the owned motion is in the loaded
  list (`MotionModule::is_anim_resource`). After the swap the kind is the owned one, so nothing repeats. No work
  variables are written. The rapid continue, exit and combo decisions remain the common status code.
- Open (tier D): whether the common ATTACK code reaches stage 2 by re-entering the status (Init) or by an in-status
  motion change (Main). The unit handles both.

## 6. Not done, and why

- **The rapid effect loop after frame 12 (task item 3) is not decoded.** The effect script's last frame-12 instruction
  is `bl 0x79e20` (at 0xa1194), followed by `b 0xa090c` (back to the start of the script). Decoding 0x79e20 shows a
  different body: `frame(2)`, `LANDING_EFFECT(sys_run_smoke)`, `frame(10)`, `EFFECT_FLIP(miifighter_mach punch_splash)`,
  `frame(11)`, `EFFECT_FOLLOW_FLIP(miifighter_mach punch_arc)`, `frame(12)`, `LANDING_EFFECT(sys_atk_smoke)`. The
  arguments of the `bl` are passed on the stack (a variadic-style call), so 0x79e20 may be a thunk that the decoder
  reads as a script body, or a separate script. Resolving that needs the registration of 0x79e20 and the thunk's
  callers, which is not done. Consequence: the unit plays the rapid effect once (frames 0-12), not in the donor's
  loop. The loop is not invented in the unit.
- **The rapid-effect jump-table is therefore not in the unit** (the loop-back `b 0xa090c` is the only jump; the
  `b`-trampolines after it are unwind landing pads).
