Background. Mii Gunner has four special slots (neutral, side, up, down) with three
vanilla variants each, twelve moves. The mod's first move library is exactly these
twelve. Before anyone designs how a slot could take a different move, we need a
file-level inventory of what one variant is made of.

One goal: for each of the twelve Gunner special variants, list the game-file
resources that belong to it, from files, with hashes. Inventory task. Do not
propose how to swap moves and do not analyse native code.
