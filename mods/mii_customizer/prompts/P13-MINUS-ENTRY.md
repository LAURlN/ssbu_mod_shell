What exists and works on the console (tier A, evidence/2026-10-07_session3/RESULT_boot2.md):
the Mii editor's edit menu (rows Headgear, Outfit, ... and the Try/Random buttons) runs the
Lua chunk ui/script_patch/chara_make/make_mii_edit_actor.lc. The mod ships a replaced chunk
(source tools/lua-src/make_mii_edit_actor_entry.lua, generated by make_entry_source.py,
compiled with tools/luac53-game; read tools/lua-src/README.md): when INPUT_FAVORITE is pressed
while the cursor is on the Headgear row it sets `self.exit_value = 7` and leaves the menu.
The plugin (plugin/src/editor.rs, `before_dispatch`) sees 7 in the native dispatcher
0x180a030, rewrites it to 1 and opens its menu on the native headgear list. Value 7 without
the plugin is harmless (tier A). Values >= 9 trap. A second way in exists in the plugin and
has never been exercised: value 1 while ZL or ZR is held, read through nn::hid
(`zl_or_zr_held`).

What the user wants: "the custom menu should open with - (Minus) anywhere in the Mii editor
instead of ZL [= the Favourite button on the Headgear row]". Coordinator's reading: on the

the Headgear row. Sub-screens (headgear list, outfit, name entry...) are out of scope.
